ERRAN BOYY – SERVICE POLICY

Reliable People. Trusted Service.

1. Purpose

This policy defines the standards, responsibilities, and terms governing all services provided by Erran Boyy. It ensures consistency, professionalism, safety, and accountability across caregiving, security, driving, and tutoring services.

2. Service Coverage

Erran Boyy provides:

  • Caregiving Services: Personal care, elderly assistance, and daily living support
  • Security Services: Property and personal protection
  • Driving Services: Professional drivers for private and corporate needs
  • Tutoring Services: Academic instruction and learning support

All personnel are screened, trained, and assigned based on client needs.

3. Professional Standards

All Erran Boyy personnel must:

  • Maintain professional conduct, punctuality, and discipline
  • Perform duties with care, competence, and diligence
  • Respect client property, privacy, and instructions
  • Avoid misconduct, negligence, or unethical behavior

Any violation will result in immediate disciplinary action or termination.

4. Client Obligations

  • Provide accurate and complete service requirements
  • Ensure a safe and respectful working environment
  • Make timely payments as agreed
  • Disclose any risks, special needs, or conditions relevant to the service

Failure to meet these obligations may result in service withdrawal.

5. Booking and Scheduling

  • Services must be scheduled in advance, except in urgent cases
  • Cancellations or rescheduling require minimum 24 hours’ notice
  • Late cancellations may attract applicable charges

6. Payment Terms

  • Fees are agreed prior to service delivery
  • Payments must be made promptly (daily, weekly, or monthly as agreed)
  • Outstanding payments may lead to immediate suspension of services

7. Safety and Liability

  • Erran Boyy is committed to maintaining high safety standards
  • While due diligence is applied in staff selection and training, the company is not liable for incidents beyond reasonable control
  • All incidents must be reported immediately for proper action

8. Confidentiality and Data Protection

  • All client information is strictly confidential
  • Personnel must not disclose, share, or misuse client information under any circumstance
  • Breach of confidentiality will result in strict disciplinary measures

9. Complaints and Resolution

  • Complaints must be reported promptly to management
  • All issues will be handled professionally, fairly, and without delay
  • Persistent or unresolved issues may result in termination of service

10. Termination of Services

Erran Boyy reserves the right to terminate services where:

  • There is policy violation
  • Payments are defaulted
  • There is abuse, unsafe conditions, or misconduct

Clients may terminate services with proper notice as agreed.

11. Policy Review

Erran Boyy reserves the right to review and update this policy at any time. Updated versions will be communicated where necessary.
